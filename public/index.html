Questions

Good to know

Do I need my own WhatsApp Business number?

No. In beta, messages go out from the CartNudge number with your store's name in the text. You can connect your own WhatsApp Business API number in settings any time.

Where does the online payment go?

Straight to your Razorpay account. CartNudge creates a payment link with your keys and never touches the money.

Does CartNudge cancel orders automatically?

No. When a buyer taps cancel, the order is tagged and a note says "do not ship". You stay in control of what gets cancelled.

What if the buyer doesn't reply?

The order stays tagged as pending, so you can choose to call, hold or ship as usual. Nothing changes without your say.

What data do you keep?

Only what's needed: order number, amount, first name, phone and the button they tapped. Data is deleted when you uninstall, as Shopify's privacy rules require.
